## Day 2 KTM 500 (150+ miles):  
Main goal of the day was to hit Genoa Peak and BLM land. Started off on the south entrance of Genoa Peak Road by taking the Alternative route. The alt route had a great view   
![Movie](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/146810F1-FE0C-4826-AAD7-A1E6AAB06021.mov)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/C3243E32-8888-4FAD-A3C4-B4B53B4C071F.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/A276C879-7F9B-4B35-82E1-01E3CD98C870.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/0ACD6FDE-40B1-4E11-AC5D-25BAF034EA8E.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/43CEC2AC-1ADF-4B49-86BE-F4F8871C365C.jpg)  
Ice and snow started being the dominant surface after a few miles in. Had to fight the uphills to get traction. Took a long time to get around some sections as I had to take the bike off the trail and into the deeper snow to avoid the ice   
![Movie](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/D70F95BA-033E-4B6B-9669-AE67C8D1740E.mp4)  
  
Made it to the top of Genoa Peak   
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/2252ECED-4B9C-47E2-AADA-CCB6BA08834A.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/3EEAC7A1-6C7D-47DC-AFED-F70097EFD485.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/5AAD6A0C-29A9-4AF7-A828-9DB72A064449.jpg)  
Tried continuing the route to Spooner but too much snow. Saw a 6 point buck. I would later find out that this snow section was only from South Camp Peak to Duane Bliss Peak and the rest was pretty clear. Didn’t want to risk it at the time so I turned back. Fought some icy uphills on the return which wore me out. No way I would’ve wanted any bigger or heavier bike for fighting the ice. When I reached the bottom of the hill I ran into an old man on an Nordon 901 on 50/50 tires. I warned him not to even try.  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/ADC45C42-408B-4C99-A42D-DFA531FF9B3B.jpg)  
Headed to Minden to get gas. Climbed a spur trail on the way   
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/D4D82967-8B1E-4B44-8E46-901FD258F3B4.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/7257292A-C299-4661-AB29-42DF6CEEF423.jpg)  
Got on BLM land as soon as I could and was blasting down desert roads. Used spur trails to get to sunrise pass road  
![Movie](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/2BC92D93-237F-442A-9A04-EBED6BF898A6.mov)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/B6196E0D-20B6-4D85-8221-A94DEB180313.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/305E5CD8-8AA7-4075-A01C-F6FA9910C4B2.jpg)  
Made it around Lebo Spring in Pine Nut Mountains. Hit some spur trails until I found a pile of poop that was as tall as my rear wheel. Decided to take a spur trail and head back to the P-10/Sunrise pass intersection following the power lines. Kept seeing piles of poop. Came across a group of wild horses in the middle of the trail. Who stared at me, parted the road, and then ran with me for about 30 seconds. A once in a lifetime experience to be adopted by wild horses  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/E969CBDC-F750-4647-9A4D-B3A6DD93B6BD.jpeg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/CECDA122-D85D-4D03-BE5F-D45EFDA564A5.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/92FC4C9A-A1AE-4C72-B278-2CAADA5B5140.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/674B9C99-62E3-42BC-B16D-7A63CD06EBCC.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/DFE76680-C297-4E06-9A64-5751171D5F5E.jpg)  
Followed trails all the way to Carson city. An amazing ride following a river bed. Stopped and got gas in Carson City. Beautiful town and I was surprised at how nice it was. Took roads to FS41039/Kings Canyon Road. That is a MUST do trail. One of my new favorite trails of all time. Such an interesting mix of terrain  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/10459CFF-A911-4052-90CA-1E298093AE72.jpg)  
![Image](../public/adventures/lake-tahoe/7C82DBD3-EC87-4437-94A1-2C1F338365D7.jpg)  
Then proceeded to try the Genoa Peak trail again but entering from the North Side in Spooner Summit to see if I could cut through since it was later in the day. Was pretty dry for the first couple miles and a super fun climb. Got to Duane Bliss peak and the snow was still there, thought not as bad. I was running out of sunlight so I decided to turn back to not get stuck out here in the dark. Hindsight 20/20 I should’ve done this trail last in the day. Decided to climb the White Hill spur trail as one last challenge. That was a super fun side hit
